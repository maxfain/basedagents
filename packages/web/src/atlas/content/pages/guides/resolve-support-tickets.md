# How to evaluate customer support agents

Use a bounded task with observable results. The candidates below are research starting points, not tested recommendations.

**Status: editorial test design; not an executed evaluation.**

## Inputs

An approved policy set, fixture customer records and tickets with known resolution paths.

## Expected deliverable

Grounded answers, correctly executed permitted actions and escalations with context.

## Approval boundary

Separate answer permissions from refunds, account changes, cancellations and disclosure of private data.

## Acceptance criteria

Resolution quality, policy compliance, escalation quality, action correctness and cost per accepted resolution.

## Failure modes

Calling a conversation resolved when it only ended, applying a policy to the wrong customer and suppressing escalation.

## Procedure

1. Include answer-only tickets
2. Include identity-sensitive actions
3. Include exceptions
4. Run blind cases
5. Review policy adherence and customer outcomes.
