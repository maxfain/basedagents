# How to evaluate agents for account research

Use a bounded task with observable results. The candidates below are research starting points, not tested recommendations.

**Status: editorial test design; not an executed evaluation.**

## Inputs

A target-company list, research questions, allowed sources and freshness requirements.

## Expected deliverable

A sourced account brief with uncertainty labels and suggested next steps.

## Approval boundary

Keep external outreach disabled unless separately authorized. Avoid deriving sensitive personal attributes.

## Acceptance criteria

Factual accuracy, source quality, freshness, entity resolution and time saved in review.

## Failure modes

Confusing similarly named companies, treating a job posting as proof of deployed technology and inventing buying intent.

## Procedure

1. Verify company identity
2. Collect business evidence
3. Separate observation from interpretation
4. Check citations
5. Have a reviewer assess relevance.
