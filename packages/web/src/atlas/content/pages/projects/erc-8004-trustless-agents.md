Proposed identity, reputation and validation registries for agents.

## Evidence scope

Payments are explicitly outside this proposal; registration alone does not establish capability or service quality.

## What it does

ERC-8004 proposes registries for agent identity, reputation and validation. The inspected proposal is marked Draft; payments are outside its stated scope.

## What this does not establish

An identity registration is not proof of competence. Reputation entries and validation claims need their own provenance and interpretation.

## Implementation checklist

- Record chain, registry address, agent identifier, record timestamp and evidence behind each reputation or validation observation.
