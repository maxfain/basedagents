# ERC-8183 Agentic Commerce

Proposed job escrow interface with client, provider and evaluator roles.

Type: protocol · Evidence checked: 2026-11-13

**Evidence:** Published-source research; no runtime test, security audit or payment test was performed for this profile.

[Official/source URL](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8183)

## Evidence scope

Evaluator approves or rejects submitted work. The evaluator can be the client; an attestation is not automatically an independent audit.

## What it does

ERC-8183 proposes a job escrow interface with client, provider and evaluator roles. The evaluator accepts or rejects work and may be the client. The inspected proposal is Draft.

## What this does not establish

An evaluator attestation is not necessarily an independent audit. A funded escrow is not the same as accepted delivery.

## Implementation checklist

- Record job terms, amount, asset, expiry, parties, evaluator relationship and acceptance or rejection evidence.
